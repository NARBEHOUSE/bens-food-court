Draw the same restaurant from INSIDE it: its materials, colours, roof and awning, blank sign shape, counter front and

Composition (percent of the image):

Hidden funny details, small but findable: a rubber duck sitting in the sushi case among the rolls; a lucky cat holding a tiny fish; a goldfish bowl with a goldfish looking nervous; a framed drawing of a very proud cucumber.
